Have you ever felt like you were too small or insignificant to be used by God? Have you ever thought God didn't want to be bothered with your small requests? Have you ever felt like you just couldn't go on?

Today, I challenge you to look for God in the small things! He's there, He's working, and He wants you to be a part of it.

Today, I want to look at the first of three stories: Elijah and the cloud the size of a man's hand, found in 1 Kings 18:42–46.

At this point in Elijah's journey with God, he had been through many ups and downs. Life had not been easy for him, but God had been faithful.

Elijah had announced a drought according to God's word, and it came. Elijah challenged the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel and rebuilt an altar to the Lord. After preparing the sacrifice and soaking the altar with water, he prayed for God to answer by fire so the people would know that the Lord was God. And God did just that! Fire came down from heaven and consumed the sacrifice, the wood, the stones, the dust, and even the water.

After the defeat of the prophets of Baal, it was time for the drought to be over.

Elijah again believed God and had confidence in what God had promised. He went to the top of Mount Carmel, bowed down to the ground, and prayed. He then instructed his servant to go and look toward the sea.

The servant returned with the news that there was nothing.

Elijah told him to go again.

Seven times Elijah sent his servant to look toward the sea. The first six times, there was nothing to report. But on the seventh time, the servant returned with the answer:

**"There’s a cloud as small as a man’s hand coming from the sea."** (1 Kings 18:44)

God was beginning to answer in a way that most people would have overlooked. But Elijah and his servant were eagerly looking for the work of God.

The servant saw the small cloud and returned with the good news. Elijah heard the news and immediately trusted that the rain would come.

He told his servant to warn Ahab to prepare his chariot and head down before the rain stopped him.

Think about that for a moment.

A cloud the size of a man's hand was going to stop someone from traveling? No! But what was coming certainly would.

Elijah saw God at work, trusted what God had promised, and immediately took action.

What can we learn from this?

Are your eyes open to what God is doing? Are you eagerly waiting for the answer to your prayer? Are you looking closely enough to notice even the smallest sign of God's work?

Is your faith strong enough to trust God when all you can see is something small?

Sometimes we become so focused on waiting for God to do something big that we overlook the small ways He is already working. We want the storm, but we ignore the cloud. We want the finished work, but we fail to recognize the beginning.

Elijah didn't need to see the rain pouring down to trust that God was going to fulfill His word. He saw a small cloud and acted in faith.

Today, let's open our eyes to the work of God. Let's pray with faith, trust His promises, and remember that God is working even when we can't see what He's doing.

Remember, God used that small cloud to announce the coming rain that would end a drought lasting more than three years.

**What can God do with the small things in your life?**

Don't overlook the small things. You never know how God might use them to accomplish something greater than you could ever imagine.
